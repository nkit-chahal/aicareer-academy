The catalog

The internet will sell you a thousand next lectures.

They arrive with new names every quarter. They rarely ask which role you are actually trying to hold. A course is a chapter. A career is a plot — with a job at the end that has not been automated into a footnote.

The job

Some titles will fade. The work of building with models will not.

Prompt-only roles thin out. Tooling changes. What remains is the person who can pick a problem, sequence the stack, and ship something a hiring manager can open. That person still has a 2028.
